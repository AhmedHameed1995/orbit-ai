# Walkthrough script (under five minutes)

## 0:00–0:30 — framing

“This is Orbit, a rebuild of Fathom's post-meeting workspace. I deliberately stubbed
the recording bot—the brief allows that—and invested in the part users live in after
the call: finding, understanding, verifying, and sharing what happened.”

Show the populated meetings screen. Point out the weekly pulse, cross-meeting insight,
filters, and six realistic seeded calls.

## 0:30–1:15 — the case that matters

Open **Atlas Labs · Q3 implementation review**.

“This is a 62-minute call with eight people, not a tiny demo recording. The player,
speaker grid, talk-time split, actions, and notes remain usable at real-call density.”

Start playback briefly and click a linked timestamp in the summary.

## 1:15–2:05 — summary to source

Switch the summary from **General** to **Customer success**, then **Executive brief**.

“Different jobs need different compression. Every important claim can take you back to
the source moment, so the AI output is inspectable instead of being a dead document.”

Check off an action item and click its timestamp.

## 2:05–2:50 — transcript to clip

Open **Transcript**. Hover a line around 26:16 and press the plus button.

“A useful moment should not disappear inside an hour of video. Saving it adds a clip in
the right rail immediately, with the speaker and timestamp still in context.”

Click the new saved moment to jump playback to it.

## 2:50–3:25 — ask and search

Open **Ask Orbit** and choose “What could delay the launch?” Show the grounded response
and its evidence link. Then use global search for “security” and show matching calls.

## 3:25–4:10 — share with someone outside the call

Press **Share**, leave access on **Anyone with the link**, and copy the URL. Open the
link in a private/incognito window.

“The public view needs no account. It keeps the recording, key summary, and exact
moments, while removing private workspace controls.”

## 4:10–4:35 — close

“What I left out is real capture, calendar OAuth, and server-side AI generation. What I
shipped is the complete high-value loop after capture, with seeded data that exposes
the product decisions rather than hiding them behind an empty state.”

End with the public live URL and repository visible. Keep camera on for the recording.
